- **AI milestones**
    > selected landmark developments
    - **Deep Blue (IBM)**
        - chess bot
        - 1997: beats the actual world champion (G. Kasparov)
    - **DeepFace (Meta)**
        - face recognition, CNN
        - achieved 97%+ accuracy on LFW ([Labeled Faces in the Wild](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jessicali9530/lfw-dataset)) benchmark in 2014, (80-90% was before) ![face base points](../Images/face_base_points.png)
        - image recognition, ResNet 2015
    - **AlphaGo (DeepMind-Google)**
        - go program
        - Beat Lee Sedol in 2016 ![AlphaGo](../Images/alphago.png)
    - **LLMs, large language models**
        - NLP
        - transformer 2017
        - GPT models 2017-2022 ![chatGPT](../Images/chatGPT_illustration.png)
        - chatGPT 2022, added reinforcement learning and human feedback
    - **AlphaFold**
        - protein structure prediction ![AlphaFold](../Images/alphafold.png)
    - **image generation models**
        - 2021: DALL-E
        - 2023: Stable Diffusion![image](../Images/AI_generated_image_astronaut.png)
        - generative revolution
    - **multimodal systems and agentic coding**
        - 2024-2025: GPT-4 class multimodal systems ![multimodal](../Images/multimodal_systems.png)
        - RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) systems
